7–9 October 2026

Edinburgh Castle

On the 2nd of September they visited Edinburgh Castle. The posts follow the visit from the main entrance to Argyle Battery, the Governor’s House, the Royal Scots Dragoon Guards Regimental Museum, the prison and the story of Private John Tool, through Foog’s Gate to the Upper Ward, St Margaret’s Chapel, and Mons Meg, described as the world’s most famous medieval gun, with a 20-inch bore that could fire 150kg cannon balls for 2 miles (3.2km). They reached Crown Square, home to the Royal Palace, the Honours of Scotland (the Scottish Crown Jewels), the Great Hall, the Queen Anne Building and the Scottish National War Memorial, and closed with thanks to Edinburgh Castle before lunch at The Witchery.
