Trip to V&A Dundee for David Bowie: On Tour

Aelith and Lilith visited V&A Dundee for the exhibition ‘David Bowie: On Tour’ on 17 November. The posts follow them setting off for Dundee, arriving in the city, and being at the museum, with photographs and films from the exhibition, including Glamour featuring David Bowie, from a photograph taken for the Scary Monsters (and Super Creeps) album artwork by Edward Bell, 1980.
