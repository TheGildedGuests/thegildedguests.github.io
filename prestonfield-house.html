5–11 October 2026

Prestonfield House

Lunch at Prestonfield House, with John and Fae arriving (and @PierceLilholt arriving in style) for fine dining at Rhubarb Restaurant. The dining room is described as absolute luxury. Choices from the menu on the day of the visit (some not currently being served) were a starter of East Lothian courgette flower with heritage tomatoes, pine nut, nasturtium, chilled cucumber and lovage tarator; a main of St Bride’s guinea fowl breast and mousseline with black truffle, shallots, confit carrot, spring cabbage and Périgueux sauce; and a pudding of apricot and yoghurt gâteau with burnt almond crémeux and apricot and verbena sorbet. Afterwards they walked around the property.
