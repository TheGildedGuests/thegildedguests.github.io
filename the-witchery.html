8–9 October 2026

The Witchery restaurant

A quick walk from Edinburgh Castle brought them to The Witchery by the Castle for lunch in The Secret Garden, with a champagne cocktail. The menu that day (some choices not currently being served) was Findlays of Portobello haggis with pineapple chutney and potato bhaji to start; salt baked celeriac tart with new-season chanterelle mushrooms, parmesan-style vegetarian cheese and summer truffle as a main; and summer fruit pavlova with crisp meringue, raspberry and elderflower ganache, raspberry sorbet and summer berry compote for pudding. They thanked the team at The Witchery who made it happen on the day.
