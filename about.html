About us

Curated experiences in Scotland.

The Gilded Guests is a new venture built around curated visits to Scotland’s historic houses, castles, restaurants and exhibitions.

Each visit is real. It is photographed and filmed on the day, then shared with the AI-augmented characters Aelith and Lilith as your hosts.
