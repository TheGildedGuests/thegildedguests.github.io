Traquair House

John and Asha visited Traquair House, described as the oldest inhabited house in Scotland, and attended a medieval Fayre. The posts show the ground-floor Still Room, kept by the 21st Lady of Traquair, Lady Catherine Maxwell-Stuart; the High Drawing Room; the Dressing Room, offering a glimpse into domestic living of the past; and the Kings Room, called truly fit for kings. They also noted events starting 5 October, including Halloween Historical Ghost Tours.
