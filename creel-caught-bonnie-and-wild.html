Creel Caught at Bonnie & Wild

A lunch break at Bonnie & Wild on St James Cres, Edinburgh, with seafood at Creel Caught, created by MasterChef UK winner and Scotland’s first National Chef, Gary Maclean. The plates were Scottish haddock fish and chips with lemon, tartar sauce, chips and mushy peas, and a warm kiln smoked haddock potato salad with warm baby potatoes, green beans, soft egg, dill, capers and Arran mustard dressing.
